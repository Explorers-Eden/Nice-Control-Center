         Presets
        
					
        Ready-made sets of every setting below, and your own. A built-in preset sets every switch and number; your presets also bring their mob limits, junk items and save limits. Frozen chunks always stay as they are. Every setting stays editable after applying.
        
					
        
					
        Your presets
        
					
        
					
        
					
        
				
        
				
        
				
        
					
         Step down while it lags
        
					
        When ticks take longer than the target, the server saves work step by step, what players notice least first: the ranges of Tick less where nobody is and Lighter mob AI shrink to the lowest you set below (three quick steps), then the mob cap and the simulation distance come down taking turns, then (with More steps first) how far players see mobs, and last the view distance. A step every 10 seconds, every 5 while ticks take more than twice the target. Once it has been calm for a minute, it undoes its steps one by one in reverse, so the far-mob ranges come back last. Nothing is saved: a restart starts from your normal settings.
        
					
        
					
        
						
						
						
						
						
						
						
						
					
        
				
        
				
        
					
         Tick less where nobody is
        
					
        Mobs far from every player only move every few ticks (like Paper's entity activation range). Mobs that are falling, swimming, fighting, burning, on a lead, riding or in a raid always tick. Items, XP, minecarts and farms are never slowed down.
        
					
        
						
					
        
					
        Advanced
						
        
							
        With lag protection on, these are the lowest ranges: without lag mobs tick normally across the whole simulation distance, and while it lags the ranges shrink down to these shares of the simulation distance at that moment (at least 16 blocks). With lag protection off they always apply.
        
							
							
							
							
							
						
        
					
        
				
        
				
        
					
         Lighter mob AI
        
					
        Mobs far from every player decide what to do next only every few ticks; what they're already doing keeps running (like Pufferfish). While the server lags, it also limits how many mobs may search a new path per tick; the others try again a moment later.
        
					
        
						
						
					
        
					
        Advanced
						
        
							
							
							
						
        
					
        
				
        
				
        
					
         Mob pushing
        
					
        In crowded pens and farms every mob pushes every other one, which grows fast. With this, a mob pushes at most a few others per tick (Paper does 8). Players always push and get pushed; cramming damage stays as it is.
        
					
        
						
						
					
        
				
        
				
        
					
         Merge dropped items and XP
        
					
        Items and XP orbs on the ground join up from farther away, so fewer of them need ticking. Vanilla merges within half a block.
        
					
        
						
						
						
						
					
        
				
        
				
        
					
         Junk despawns sooner
        
					
        Dropped items on this list (cobblestone, netherrack, rotten flesh…) disappear after the time below instead of 5 minutes. Everything else keeps the full 5 minutes. Arrows shot by mobs, which nobody can pick up, also leave the ground sooner.
        
					
        
						
						
						
					
        
					
        
					
        
				
        
				
        
					
         Save limits per chunk
        
					
        When a chunk is saved, only this many of each kind are kept; the rest are gone when it loads again (Paper's entity per chunk save limit). Stops arrows, XP orbs and snowballs from piling up over months. While the chunk stays loaded nothing changes.
        
					
        
						
					
        
					
        
					
        
				
        
				
        
					
         Breeding caps
        
					
        Stops animal and villager farms from growing without end: no breeding while too many of the same kind live nearby.
        
					
        
						
						
						
						
					
        
				
        
				
        
					
         Trading halls
        
					
        Villagers that can't move (1×1 cells, minecarts) run their brain only every few ticks. Trading and restocking keep working.
        
					
        
						
						
					
        
				
        
				
        
					
         Hoppers
        
					
        A hopper with nothing to move looks again every tick. With this it waits a few ticks after finding nothing. Hoppers that move items keep their normal speed, and locked hoppers behave as in vanilla, so redstone timing stays the same.
        
					
        
						
						
					
        
				
        
				
        
					
         Spawn less often
        
					
        Vanilla tries to spawn monsters, water mobs and bats in every chunk near players every tick (animals only every 20 seconds). Trying every few ticks instead (like Paper's ticks-per spawns) costs much less; caps still fill up, only a little slower. Each dimension tries on its own ticks. Needs mod 1.23.0.
        
					
        
						
						
						
						
					
        
				
        
				
        
					
         Armor stands
        
					
        Armor stands that stand still update only once a second (like Paper's armor stands tick: false). Hit, pushed, falling, burning or ridden ones update normally.
        
					
        
						
					
        
				
        
				
        
					
         Saving and teleports
        
					
        Smooth saving: the autosave every 5 minutes writes changed chunks and player files over the next seconds instead of all in one tick, which removes its lag spike. save-all, stopping and backups still save everything at once. Warm teleport targets: places players teleport to three times in a day (hubs, homes, spawn) stay loaded, without ticking, so arriving there doesn't load them first. They're listed under Loaded chunks as Teleport target, at most 24.
        
					
        
						
						
					
        
				
        
				
        
					
         Mob limits per chunk
        
					
        How many mobs of a kind may live in one chunk. Used by Clean up now below, by the Clean up step in Schedule and, when switched on, every few seconds on their own. Pick single mobs or groups (all animals, all monsters…); a single mob's limit wins over its group's. Named, tamed, leashed and persistent mobs are never removed.

         Borders
        
					
        Per dimension, distances in blocks from the center. Inside the soft border is the permanent area: it's never trimmed. Between the soft and the hard border lie the outer wilds, which a scheduled task can reset (trim) every week. Nobody gets past the hard border: players are put back inside, and a square one also sets the world border so they see the wall. Hard border 0 = none.

         Pregenerate
        
					
        Generates every chunk up to the hard border (or the soft border without one) in each dimension with Pregen ticked, one dimension after another, so players don't wait for terrain and the map is complete. It runs on its own thread in the mod, only as fast as the server has room for, keeps going with nobody online and carries on after a restart.

        Deletes all generated chunks outside the soft border in every dimension with borders (whole region files, single chunks along the edge, also entities and points of interest). They generate fresh when someone goes there. Only while the server is stopped. To do this every week, add a task in Schedule with the Reset the outer wilds step (it stops and starts the server itself), or use the preset there.

            2.

           Channel topic
          
					
          Keeps a channel's topic up to date with who's online and, with Nice Actions installed, its Time/Date HUD (in-game time, date and weekday). Discord allows only two topic changes per 10 minutes, so it changes at most every 5 minutes. The bot needs Manage Channels there.
          
					
          
						
						
					
          
					
					
					
          
				
          
				
          
					
           Account linking
          
					
          Players post a code once, in the link channel or as a direct message to the bot; after that they can join. In the link channel the bot removes the message right away and answers by direct message (or, when those are closed, with a mention that disappears after 15 seconds). unlink removes the link the same way.
          
					
          
						
						
						
					
          
					
          
						
					
          
					
          Roles for linked players
          
					
          The bot gives every linked player the role below, plus the role of their Fabled Roots race (from the race tag, checked every 5 minutes), and swaps or takes them away when the race changes or they unlink. Empty = no role. Right-click a role → Copy Role ID. The bot needs Manage Roles, and its own role must be above these roles.
          
					
          
						
					
          
					
          
					
				
          
				
          
					
           Events & alerts
          
					
          Tick what gets posted, pick the channel, and change the wording. {player}, {message} and {advancement} are filled in; deaths and advancements follow the game rules showDeathMessages and announceAdvancements (Fabled Roots' own death messages count as deaths). "/tellraw @a and /say messages" are what data packs (or admins) send to everyone with /tellraw @a, plus /say; the same text twice within a minute is skipped, and at most 20 go out per minute. Alerts also show in the panel's console; lag alerts come at most every 15 minutes, disk and memory every 6 hours.
