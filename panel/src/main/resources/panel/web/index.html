        Your presets
        
					
        
					
        
					
        
				
        
				
        
				
        
					
         Step down while it lags
        
					
        When ticks run over the target, the server saves work step by step, what players notice least first: the far-mob ranges below shrink, then the mob cap and the simulation distance take turns, and last the view distance. A step every 10 seconds (5 when it's bad); after a calm minute the steps are undone in reverse. A restart starts from your normal settings.
        
					
        
					
        
						
						
						
						
						
						
						
						
					
        
				
        
				
        
					
         Tick less where nobody is
        
					
        Mobs far from every player only move every few ticks (like Paper's entity activation range). Mobs that are falling, swimming, fighting, burning, on a lead, riding or in a raid always tick. Items, XP, minecarts and farms are never slowed down.
        
					
        
						
					
        
					
        Advanced
						
        
							
        With Step down while it lags on, these are the lowest the ranges go while it lags (without lag, mobs tick normally). Without it they always apply. At least 16 blocks.
        
							
							
							
							
							
						
        
					
        
				
        
				
        
					
         Lighter mob AI
        
					
        Mobs far from every player decide what to do next only every few ticks; what they're already doing keeps running (like Pufferfish), brains of villagers, piglins and the like included. Tamed mobs always think. While it lags, only so many mobs may search a new path per tick.
        
					
        
						
						
					
        
					
        Advanced
						
        
							
							
							
						
        
					
        
				
        
				
        
					
         Mob pushing
        
					
        In crowded pens and farms every mob pushes every other one, which grows fast. With this, a mob pushes at most a few others per tick (Paper does 8). Players always push and get pushed; cramming damage stays as it is.
        
					
        
						
						
					
        
				
        
				
        
					
         Merge dropped items and XP
        
					
        Items and XP orbs on the ground join up from farther away, so fewer of them need ticking. Vanilla merges within half a block.
        
					
        
						
						
						
						
					
        
				
        
				
        
					
         Junk despawns sooner
        
					
        Dropped items on this list (cobblestone, netherrack, rotten flesh…) disappear after the time below instead of 5 minutes. Everything else keeps the full 5 minutes. Arrows shot by mobs, which nobody can pick up, also leave the ground sooner.
        
					
        
						
						
						
					
        
					
        
					
        
				
        
				
        
					
         Save limits per chunk
        
					
        When a chunk is saved, only this many of each kind are kept; the rest are gone when it loads again (Paper's entity per chunk save limit). Stops arrows, XP orbs and snowballs from piling up over months. While the chunk stays loaded nothing changes.
        
					
        
						
					
        
					
        
					
        
				
        
				
        
					
         Per dimension
        
					
        Make single worlds light, like a minigame world: no mobs spawning on their own, no random ticks (crops, leaves, ice), everything standing still while nobody is there, dropped items gone sooner, a sky that stays clear when it rains elsewhere. A world left as it is costs nothing extra.
        
					
        DimensionMobs spawnRandom ticksFreeze when emptyItems gone after (s, 0 = 5 min)Always clear sky
        
					
        
				
        
				
        
					
         Breeding caps
        
					
        Stops animal and villager farms from growing without end: no breeding while too many of the same kind live nearby.
        
					
        
						
						
						
						
					
        
				
        
				
        
					
         Trading halls
        
					
        Villagers that can't move (1×1 cells, minecarts) run their brain only every few ticks. Trading and restocking keep working.
        
					
        
						
						
					
        
				
        
				
        
					
         Hoppers
        
					
        A hopper with nothing to move waits a few ticks before it looks again, instead of every tick. Moving hoppers keep their speed and locked ones behave as in vanilla, so redstone timing and item filters still work.
        
					
        
						
						
					
        
				
        
				
        
					
         Spawn less often
        
					
        Vanilla tries to spawn monsters, water mobs and bats every tick. Trying every few ticks (like Paper's ticks-per spawns) costs much less; caps still fill up, only a little slower.
        
					
        
						
						
						
						
					
        
				
        
				
        
					
         Armor stands
        
					
        Armor stands that stand still update only once a second (like Paper's armor stands tick: false). Hit, pushed, falling, burning or ridden ones update normally.

         Dungeons
        
					
        Dungeons you build yourself. Every player or party gets a fresh copy in the dungeon dimension when they step on a pad; it's wiped again when they're done.
        
					
        
						
        Build
        Anywhere; then /dungeon create <name> at one corner, /dungeon corner2 at the other, /dungeon save <name> (again after changes)
        
						
        Markers
        Data structure blocks with the text start (required), exit, checkpoint, mob:zombie x3, boss:wither_skeleton {CustomName:'"Warden of Bones"'}; chests with a loot table roll fresh every run
        
						
        Getting the blocks
        /dungeon tool marker start (any marker text), /dungeon tool door <name>, /dungeon tool spot <group> <door> [turns into]: ready-made blocks, placed in creative
        
						
        Random rooms
        Build a room, /dungeon room create <name> + /dungeon corner2, /dungeon room save <name> [group]. Its doors are jigsaw blocks facing out. A spot (a jigsaw whose target pool is a group) gets a random room of that group, turned so a door with the spot's target name faces it. A filled spot becomes what the door turns into (the doorway), an empty one its own "turns into" block (a wall). Rooms can have spots of their own; they always stay inside the dungeon's box. Weights and chances are set below.
        
						
        Pools and pads
        /dungeon pad add <pool> where players should stand; a run picks a random dungeon of that pool
        
						
        Parties
        /party invite <player>; the leader stands on the pad and members within 16 blocks come along
        
						
        Kit
        /dungeon kit <name> makes what you carry the dungeon's kit
        
						
        Rewards
        "Clears a dungeon" and "Fails a dungeon", with {score} (the time) and {game}

         Own commands
        
					
        Commands of your own: a link answers with a clickable address (/discord), a text with a few lines (/rules), an alias runs other commands (/spawn). Each can have more names and be limited to operators or a permission node. A name another command already uses is skipped. They change in game within a few seconds of saving.
        
					
        
						
					
        
				
        
				
        
				
        
			
        

			
        
				
        
					
         Rewards
        
					
        Run commands and send a message when something happens: a player's first join, every login, a first trip to the Nether, an advancement, a level, an in-game time or date (the Nice Actions clock and calendar when the pack is installed), or simply every few minutes. Each rule can be for everyone or for one player, and run every time, once per player or once at all.
        
					
        Variables: {player} {uuid} {x} {y} {z} {dimension} {value} (the dimension, advancement, mob, level…) {day} {time} {date} {online} {rewards} (what the rule gave). Commands run with full rights, at the player, so @s and ~ ~ ~ mean them.

           Account linking

          Roles for linked players
          
					
          The bot gives every linked player the role below, plus the role of their Fabled Roots race (from the race tag, checked every 5 minutes), and swaps or takes them away when the race changes or they unlink. Empty = no role. Right-click a role → Copy Role ID. The bot needs Manage Roles, and its own role must be above these roles.
